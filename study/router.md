**Anchors not served:** Baylor University (Waco).

Line planning (`vendor/dfw-transit/metro/lineplan.py`): after the full model prunes the greedy's pool, a local search on the same model improves the network as a whole: it merges downtown stubs into through lines, adds or swaps lines to reach unserved anchors, drops lines other lines make redundant, swaps a weak line for a better version of its corridor and sets each line's headway, maximizing new daily trips less 1 trip per $M of cost (the pruning's floor) less a penalty for each unserved anchor. Routes between centers also pass anchors no center stands on (a campus beside downtown), and a corridor dense enough for metro where no freeway or major arterial runs gets BRT on its street. The comparison above is against the networks before the line planner when run with that folder. What's not done: turn penalties in the road router (edge-based routing), and an exact integer program in place of the local search.
