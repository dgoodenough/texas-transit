# Houston and the policies

Generated by `python study/houston_policies.py`. The model test (`study/metro_potential.md`) found Houston's transit share falling when the 2 million plan's policies were switched on. Here the network is held fixed (the default generated network at DFW's 75-year spending) and DFW Transit's demand model runs on it with each policy alone, then all of them, on today's land use. Austin is the control.
