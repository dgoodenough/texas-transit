## What the review changes

Every project passes gate 2 as scheduled: the whole map's schedule applies the gate itself (`study/timing.py` GATE_YEARS).

16 of 23 projects fail the stress gate without a designation: every one that isn't in the Connectivity Program (Texas Central, I-35 north, I-35 south, I-10, Dallas - Tyler - Longview - Shreveport, Fort Worth - Dallas, Gulf Coast, Houston - Victoria - Corpus Christi, US 290, Sunset, I-20, Heartland Flyer, Oklahoma City - Wichita - Kansas City, Texas Eagle east, El Paso - Las Cruces - Albuquerque, San Antonio - Laredo - Monterrey). Read strictly, the gate would stop them all, the Triangle included: at cost x1.45 and riders x0.70 no corridor in the plan clears 0.8 (`study/hsr_buildout.md`, Phase 1 results). Building them is a policy choice made on the central case and its P10-P90 range (`study/timing.md`, How sure), which the decade review should re-make each decade at current costs and riders, not a sure investment.
