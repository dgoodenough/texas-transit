## What the review changes

2 project(s) start before the corridor they hang off has run 3 years: the schedule lets a branch be built alongside its trunk to open no earlier than it, which the gate doesn't allow. Held to the gate, each starts 3 years after its trunk opens: Oklahoma City - Wichita - Kansas City (starts 2061, held to 2067), Texas Eagle east (starts 2062, held to 2067).

15 of 23 projects fail the stress gate without a designation: every one that isn't in the Connectivity Program (Texas Central, I-35 north, I-35 south, I-10, Fort Worth - Dallas, Dallas - Tyler - Longview - Shreveport, Gulf Coast, US 290, Heartland Flyer, Oklahoma City - Wichita - Kansas City, I-20, Texas Eagle east, Sunset, El Paso - Las Cruces - Albuquerque, San Antonio - Laredo - Monterrey). Under the governor's rule (`study/second_session.md`, item 2) each gets its right-of-way protected and its design done, and no construction money, until it passes the gate at current costs and riders or the Legislature designates it by name. Read strictly, the gate would stop them all, the Triangle included: at cost x1.45 and riders x0.70 no corridor in the plan clears 0.8 (`study/hsr_buildout.md`, Phase 1 results), so the designations are where the decisions are made.

## The binding gate (third session)

The governor's rule since the third session (`study/third_session.md`, items 1 and 10): every corridor and every metro line keeps its place in the plan and gets its design and right-of-way, but signs construction only when its refreshed cost estimate (at 30% design) and refreshed forecast clear 0.8 under the stress; a metro line also waits until its city has zoned its station districts by right, and the refreshed forecast uses the zoning the city has actually adopted. The money of a project that fails waits in the fund for the next decade's review. The **To pass the gate** column says how much better than today's estimate a project's numbers must come in.

**The metro lines** under the same gate: 1 of 80 pass (I-35-San Jacinto BRT). The median of the rest needs costs 48% under the estimate or riders 91% over it.
