## What the review changes

2 project(s) start before the corridor they hang off has run 3 years: the schedule lets a branch be built alongside its trunk to open no earlier than it, which the gate doesn't allow. Held to the gate, each starts 3 years after its trunk opens: Oklahoma City - Wichita - Kansas City (starts 2064, held to 2070), Texas Eagle east (starts 2064, held to 2070).

16 of 23 projects fail the stress gate without a designation: every one that isn't in the Connectivity Program (Texas Central, I-35 north, I-35 south, I-10, Dallas - Tyler - Longview - Shreveport, Fort Worth - Dallas, Gulf Coast, Houston - Victoria - Corpus Christi, US 290, El Paso - Las Cruces - Albuquerque, Heartland Flyer, Oklahoma City - Wichita - Kansas City, I-20, Texas Eagle east, Sunset, San Antonio - Laredo - Monterrey). Under the governor's rule (`study/second_session.md`, item 2) each gets its right-of-way protected and its design done, and no construction money, until it passes the gate at current costs and riders or the Legislature designates it by name. Read strictly, the gate would stop them all, the Triangle included: at cost x1.45 and riders x0.70 no corridor in the plan clears 0.8 (`study/hsr_buildout.md`, Phase 1 results), so the designations are where the decisions are made.
